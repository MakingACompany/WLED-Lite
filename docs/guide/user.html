1. Getting to the controls

Your installer set up your sign on your home Wi-Fi and gave you a web address to open in your phone's browser. It usually looks like one of these:

  • A name: your-sign.local (or whatever your installer named it)
  • A number: 192.168.1.42 (or similar)

Open that address in Safari (iPhone) or Chrome (Android). The main control screen will appear.

Tip: Add this page to your phone's home screen so it opens with one tap. In Safari, tap the share button → Add to Home Screen. In Chrome, tap the three dots → Add to Home Screen.

2. The main screen

When you open the page, you'll see a calm dark background with a few rounded "cards." Each card controls one thing about your sign.

  1. Section (only on multi-letter signs) — pick which letter to control.
  2. Power — turns the sign on and off.
  3. Brightness — how bright the lights are.
  4. Color — picks the color the sign glows.
  5. Effect — how the lights move or shimmer.
  6. Turn off in — auto-shutoff after a set time.
  7. Daily schedule — turns on / off the same times every day.
The little gear icon in the top-right corner is for your installer. If you tap it, you'll see a request for a PIN. That's normal — you don't need to go in there.

3. Turning the sign on and off

The big round button at the top is your power button.

  • Tap it once to turn the sign on.
  • Tap it again to turn the sign off.

When the sign is on, a soft amber glow appears around the button and the word underneath changes from Off to On.

4. Adjusting brightness

The slider sets how bright the whole sign is — from a soft nightlight glow to full daylight visibility.

  • Slide left for dimmer.
  • Slide right for brighter.
  • The percentage on the right updates as you slide.

Brightness changes apply to the whole sign at once, even if your sign has multiple letters or sections.

5. Changing the color

The color wheel lets you pick any color for the sign.

  • Tap or drag anywhere on the wheel.
  • The sign updates right away — there's no "save" button needed.

For pure white, tap right in the center. For pastels, drag toward the center; for vivid colors, drag toward the edge.

Try it: the color wheel in the picture is live — go ahead, drag it.

6. Picking an effect

An "effect" changes how the lights behave. Some effects keep the color steady (like Solid — just a flat color). Others make the lights breathe, twinkle, chase along the strip, or pulse like firelight.

Tap any name to switch to that effect. The currently active effect glows amber.

A few favorites to try

  • Solid — steady, no movement. Good for daytime.
  • Breathe — gentle slow fade in and out.
  • Twinkle — soft random sparkles, like fireflies.
  • Fire 2012 — warm flickering, like candles.
  • Sunrise — slowly brightens from dark to bright. Nice as a wake-up effect.

7. "Turn off in…" — auto-shutoff timer

Use this when you want the sign on for a while and then off — without remembering to come back and turn it off.

Tap one of:

  • Never — stays on until you turn it off.
  • 1 hr / 4 hr / 12 hr — fades off automatically after that many hours.

Tap Never to cancel.

8. Setting a daily schedule

If you want the sign to turn itself on and off at the same times every day — for example, on at 6 pm and off at midnight — use this card.

How to set it

  1. Tap On at and pick the time you want the sign to come on each day.
  2. Tap Off at and pick the time you want it to go off.
  3. Tap Save schedule.

A confirmation message appears underneath. The sign will now turn itself on and off at those times every day.

To change it later: repeat the same steps with new times and tap Save again — the new schedule replaces the old one.

To remove the schedule: tap Clear.

Important: the schedule depends on the sign knowing what time it is, which it gets from the internet. If your Wi-Fi or internet is down, the schedule won't work until it comes back. The sign normally syncs its clock every five minutes.

9. Multi-letter signs (Sections)

If your sign has more than one letter or region — for example a L-A-B-B sign — you'll see an extra card at the top called Section.

  • All (selected by default) — color and effect changes apply to the whole sign.
  • L, A, B₁, B₂, etc. — tap a letter to change only that letter.

When two or more letters share the same letter (like two B's), they're labeled B₁ and B₂ with small subscript numbers so they're easy to tell apart.

Switching back to controlling the whole sign: tap All.

Brightness always applies to the whole sign — that's a global setting.

Longer signs

For longer signs with five or more sections (a word like WELCOME), the Section row collapses so it fits nicely on your screen as [All] [Letters ▸]. Tap Letters to open a drawer with individual letter pills. Tap All to close it.

A 4-letter sign (top) and a 7-letter sign with its Letters drawer open (bottom).

10. Troubleshooting

The page won't load

  • Make sure your phone is connected to the same Wi-Fi network as the sign — your home Wi-Fi, not a guest network or your phone's cellular data.
  • Try the other address your installer gave you. Sometimes the name (your-sign.local) works and sometimes the number address works better.
  • If the sign was unplugged or just turned back on, wait about a minute and try again.

"Reconnecting…" message at the top

Your phone briefly lost contact with the sign. It usually clears on its own within a few seconds. If it stays for more than a minute, refresh the page.

The daily schedule isn't firing

The schedule uses internet time. If your internet has been down, give it five minutes after the connection is back, then check the schedule. If the times still look wrong, contact your installer.

Colors look different than I picked

LED brightness affects how colors appear — at very low brightness, deep reds and blues can look washed out. Try raising the brightness slider. If the issue stays, contact your installer.

Something else seems wrong

Contact your installer — they have a settings menu (behind that gear icon) where they can check on things and reset the sign if needed.

A note on power

  • Don't unplug and re-plug rapidly. Wait 10 seconds between plug cycles.
  • The sign will return to its last state when power comes back on — same color, same effect, same brightness.
  • If you have a smart outlet or wall switch, you can use that to cut power overnight. The sign saves your settings and resumes when power returns.
